Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StudySprint handles information when you use this website. StudySprint is a student project created for educational purposes.

1. Information collected

StudySprint does not ask you to create an account and does not intentionally send your task details, quiz answers, or study activity to a server. Tasks and quiz records that you save are stored locally in your browser using local storage.

2. How information is used

Information saved in local storage is used only to display your tasks, progress, and quiz record within this browser. The website does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or a remote database in this version.

3. Storage, sharing, and deletion

Locally stored information is not automatically synced to another browser or device. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to view the saved information. You can remove it by deleting tasks within the app or clearing this website's local storage through your browser settings.

4. Security and limitations

Local storage is convenient for a demonstration project but is not designed for sensitive or confidential information. Do not enter passwords, private records, or other sensitive data into StudySprint.

5. Third-party services

The website's core features run in your browser. The public hosting provider may process basic technical information when you visit the deployed website, according to that provider's own policies.

6. Contact

For questions about this student project or its privacy practices, contact the student developer through the contact method provided by your course instructor. No contact details are collected by this website.

Before publishing, review this policy and update it if you add accounts, analytics, a database, or other third-party services.
